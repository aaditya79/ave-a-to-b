Ave A to B: safer bike commutes in NYC

Enter where you start and where you're going. A team of AI agents finds three routes (fastest, balanced, and lowest risk), checks police-reported bike crashes since 2021 on each, adjusts for how many people ride there, and tells you what the safer route costs in minutes. It also flags crash hotspots and checks what happened after new bike lanes went in.

For NYC cyclists picking a commute, new Citi Bike or e-bike riders, and students. Answers take one to three minutes.
