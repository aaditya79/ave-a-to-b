Ave A to B

Which way should I bike?

Ave A to B compares three NYC bike routes by crash risk, adjusted for how many people ride each street.

What it explores

Police-reported crashes that hurt a cyclist on every street since 2021, weighed against how many people ride each one, so a busy lane isn't mistaken for a dangerous one.

Who it's for

Anyone who bikes in New York City and wants to know what a calmer route costs in minutes, from daily commuters to someone planning a first ride to work.

How to use it

Enter where you start and where you're going, pick when you ride, and press Compare routes. AI agents check three routes in about a minute. Or tap an example below to see a saved result right away.
